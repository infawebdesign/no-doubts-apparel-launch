# No Doubts Apparel Launch

Create a complete, responsive ecommerce website for a clothing brand called No Doubts Apparel.

No Doubts Apparel is a new apparel/streetwear brand with a strong fitness and gym influence. The brand’s existing designs feature bold typography, weightlifting imagery, tigers, snakes, dumbbells, and graphic-heavy T-shirt artwork.

The website should feel like a real brand experience, not a generic Shopify-style ecommerce template.

Build the following pages:

- Home

- Shop

- Individual product pages

- About

- Contact

There will initially be 4 products.

Some initial product mockups and apparel artwork are already available and should be used throughout the website. Current usable product imagery includes:

- Tiger / “Earn Your Stripes” tee

- Snake + dumbbells / “Hit The Standard” tee

- “Leave No Doubts” script tee

The fourth product does not yet have final imagery, so use a clear placeholder for that product only.

Additional and final product photography will be provided later. Structure all product image areas and galleries so the current mockups can easily be replaced or expanded with final photography later without redesigning the pages.

The final product descriptions are still being prepared, so use temporary placeholder copy where necessary and do not invent detailed product specifications, materials, sizing information, or other facts that have not been provided.

The final About/brand copy is also still being prepared, so create the full About page layout and structure now, but use clearly identifiable placeholder copy where needed rather than inventing a detailed brand story.

The website will eventually integrate with Square.

Square will be the central source of truth for:

- Products

- Pricing

- Inventory

- Orders

- Payments

Customers should browse products and product pages on the custom No Doubts Apparel website, then use Square’s secure checkout to complete their purchase.

Do not build or fake the live Square integration yet. Instead, structure the frontend so Square catalog data, inventory, pricing, and checkout can be connected later without requiring a major redesign.

The website must be fully responsive and polished across:

- Desktop

- Tablet

- Mobile

Focus heavily on:

- Strong brand presentation

- Product-focused layouts

- High-impact typography

- Strong campaign/editorial sections

- Clean and easy navigation

- Clear calls to action

- A polished shopping experience

- Visually strong product pages

- A design system that fits the gym/streetwear identity

Avoid:

- Generic SaaS styling

- Overly rounded cards

- Excessive gradients

- Fake testimonials

- Fake reviews

- Fake statistics

- Fake policies

- Fake contact information

- Fake product details

- Invented claims about the company

Use the provided branding, artwork, logo files, and product mockups as the visual foundation for the site.

Build the complete frontend structure and all pages now, while keeping any unfinished client content clearly replaceable later.
